Deployment notes

Runs on any Linux SBC or mini PC. The box serves Wi-Fi (hostapd), DHCP (dnsmasq), routing and shaping.

The local router driver needs nftables, iproute2 and root (systemd unit handles that).

Set the coin driver to gpio in /etc/bagsik/bagsik.env once the acceptor is wired.
