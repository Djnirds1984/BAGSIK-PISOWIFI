How the ports are used

eth0 carries the internet. Every other wired port is a hotspot server: a switch, an access point in bridge mode, or a direct cable to a PC.

Each customer session gets an accept rule in the forward chain and a shaped class on every hotspot port, so moving a device from eth1 to eth2 keeps its speed limit.

All ports share one subnet ({{ address }}/24), which is what lets dnsmasq hand out one DHCP range. Wi-Fi still comes from the radio the installer configures (hostapd).

Saving here overrides BAGSIK_LAN_IFACE={{ env_lan }} and BAGSIK_WAN_IFACE={{ env_wan }}; those stay as the fallback for a box that has never been configured.
